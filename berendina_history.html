Chapter One

Where It All Began

The roots of Berendina trace back to the early 1980s, when a Dutch woman named Berendina Borst visited rural Sri Lanka for the first time.

During her journey in 1982, she travelled to the village of Garagoda in Yatiyantota, located in the Kegalle District of Sri Lanka.

What she witnessed there changed her life forever.

  • Families were trapped in severe poverty, many living in fragile houses
  • Access to sanitation, healthcare, clean water, and stable income was severely limited
  • Children struggled with malnutrition and educational hardships

For many visitors, such realities become temporary memories. For Berendina Borst, they became a lifelong mission.

✦
Chapter Two

The Beginning of a Human Connection

In Garagoda, Berendina met a Sri Lankan social worker and teacher named Eva Mudalige, who was already working closely with vulnerable rural families.

That meeting became the foundation of a partnership built not on charity alone, but on dignity, trust, and community empowerment.

Together, they began supporting families through small but meaningful interventions:

  • Repairing damaged houses and improving sanitation
  • Supporting nutrition and healthcare needs
  • Helping vulnerable children access education
  • Strengthening rural livelihoods

The goal was never simply to "give aid." The vision was to help people rebuild stability, confidence, and hope.

✦
Chapter Three

From One Woman's Vision to an Organization

As the work expanded, it became clear that the mission needed structure and long-term sustainability.

1982
Berendina Borst visits Garagoda, Yatiyantota — a moment that changes everything.
1987
Berendina Borst establishes Berendina Stichting in the Netherlands to formally continue the development work in Sri Lanka.
1987 — The Same Year
Berendina Borst passes away. Her death could have ended the mission. Instead, it became the reason the mission continued.

Berendina lived on — not only as a name, but as a continuing promise to vulnerable communities.

✦
Chapter Four

The Growth of Berendina in Sri Lanka

Over time, Berendina evolved from a small village-focused initiative into one of Sri Lanka's recognized development organizations, eventually becoming formally known as Berendina Development Services (Gte) Ltd.

The organization expanded its work across multiple districts, supporting thousands of families through programs in:

Chapter Five

A Different Approach to Poverty

One reason Berendina became respected in the development sector is because of how it viewed poverty. Berendina understood that poverty is not simply about a lack of money.

Poverty can also mean:

  • A child losing access to education
  • A farmer losing crops due to climate challenges
  • A family living without clean water
  • A woman lacking opportunities for income
  • Communities being unheard and excluded

Because of this understanding, Berendina adopted a holistic approach to development — creating sustainable pathways toward dignity and self-reliance, not merely temporary relief.

✦
Chapter Six

Berendina in the Modern Era

Today, Berendina continues its work across several regions of Sri Lanka, engaging with communities affected by poverty, economic hardship, social exclusion, and vulnerability.

The organization increasingly integrates digital systems, data-driven decision making, community accountability mechanisms, and evidence-based development approaches to meet modern challenges including:

  • Climate risks and environmental shifts
  • Economic instability and migration
  • Youth unemployment and social inequality

Berendina continues adapting to these realities while staying rooted in the same core principle that started everything: Human dignity matters.
